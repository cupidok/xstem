Earthquake Data

The following dataset contains the locations of 1000 earthquakes that occurred near Fiji since 1964.

  • lat: Latitude of the earthquake.
  • long: Longitude of the earthquake.
  • depth: Vertical distance (km) of the earthquake from the earth’s surface where the fault ruptured.
  • mag: Richter magnitude of the earthquake.
  • stations: Number of stations reporting the earthquake.
